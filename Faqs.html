FAQs About Ultimate Frisbee

New to ultimate frisbee? This page covers the basics, including rules, equipment, Spirit of the Game, and how to get involved.

What is Ultimate Frisbee?

Ultimate Frisbee is a non-contact team sport played with a flying disc. The objective is to score points by catching the disc in the opposing team's end zone.

How many players are on a team?

A standard Ultimate Frisbee team consists of seven players on the field at a time. However, casual games can be played with fewer players.

How is the game started?

The game starts with a "pull," where one team throws the disc to the other team from their own end zone. This is similar to a kickoff in football.

What are the basic rules?

Players cannot run while holding the disc. The disc can be advanced by passing it to teammates. A point is scored when a player catches the disc in the opponent's end zone. If the disc is dropped, intercepted, or goes out of bounds, possession switches to the other team.

How long is a game?

Games can be played to a set number of points, typically 15, or to a certain time limit, often 90 minutes. Tournament games might have specific time constraints and scoring caps.

What is the "Spirit of the Game"?

Ultimate Frisbee emphasizes sportsmanship and fair play. Players are responsible for their own foul and line calls, and disputes are resolved on the field through discussion. This principle is known as the "Spirit of the Game."

What equipment do I need to play?

A standard 175-gram flying disc, comfortable athletic clothing, and cleats for better traction. Water and sunscreen are also important for outdoor play.

Are there any major tournaments?

Yes, there are many major tournaments, including national and international championships. The World Ultimate Club Championships and the USA Ultimate College Championships are among the most prestigious.

Can I play Ultimate Frisbee indoors?

Yes, Ultimate Frisbee can be played indoors. Indoor Ultimate is usually played on a smaller field with fewer players per team.

Where can I find a team or league to join?

Local parks, community centers, and universities often have Ultimate Frisbee teams and leagues. Online resources like Meetup, Facebook groups, and the USA Ultimate website can help you find local opportunities.

The Most Important Aspect is Spirit of the Game!

All players should compete, respect each other, communicate fairly, and have fun.
