About the Team

Drought Ultimate

Phoenix, Arizona’s competitive open division ultimate frisbee team.

Who We Are

We are Drought Ultimate, a Phoenix-based ultimate frisbee team competing in the open division. Our team represents the Southwest ultimate community through competition, teamwork, and Spirit of the Game.

We focus on athletic development, smart field movement, strong communication, and building a team culture where players can compete hard while respecting the game.

100th National Ranking
8th Southwest Region
AZ Phoenix Based

Our Team Culture

Drought Ultimate is built around effort, accountability, and trust. We believe great ultimate comes from players who are willing to learn, communicate, and support each other on and off the field.

Whether you are watching, learning about ultimate, or interested in joining the community, our goal is to represent Phoenix ultimate with pride.
